## Division with Remainder

For any integers $a, b \in Z,\quad  b \ne 0$, where $a$ is a **dividend** and $b$ is its **divisor**, it is always true that $a=bq+r, \quad q, r \in Z$, where $q$ is the **quotient** and $r$ is the **remainder**.

Additionally, $0 \le r < \left|b\right|$.

---

## Examples

Divide $17$ by $5$:

- $17=5 \times 3 + 2$
- $q=3, r=2$

Divide $-23$ by $6$:

- $-23 = 6 \times -4 + 1$
- $q=-4, r=1$
